The artist

One person, three years, five hundred faces

I'm GCAMUR, a digital artist from Türkiye. In 2023 I started making one crowned face. Then another. Then I couldn't stop.

The crowns draw on the jewelry I grew up looking at: gold filigree, turquoise, lapis, deep red stones. Every face goes through many drafts. Most never leave my folder. The ones you see here are the ones I kept.

I'm not a studio. I'm one artist building one story, chapter by chapter. If you collect a Genesis piece, you are one of the first people in that story.
