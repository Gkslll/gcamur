The city

Why Balıkesir

In 1919 and 1920, Balıkesir was one of the places where the Kuva-yi Milliye, the National Forces, organized resistance in western Anatolia. The congresses held in the city helped turn scattered local fighters into an organized defense. Today Balıkesir carries that memory in its name: the City of the National Forces.

This collection is my love letter to that city. Rainy avenues, the flag over the square, harbors at dusk, the monument lit red, the coastline from above. Every piece is one moment of Balıkesir as I see it: proud, warm and burning with evening light.

Bu koleksiyon, Kuva-yi Milliye ruhunu taşıyan Balıkesir'e bir saygı duruşu.
