About the series

Four works, no more

Most of my collections have dozens or hundreds of pieces. This one has four, and it will stay that way. I made it in 2023 as a study of one feeling: holding on to innocence when everything around you says it is pointless.

The works live on Ethereum and are available on OpenSea.
