About the series

A sacred place, painted with care

The Al-Aqsa compound in Jerusalem is one of the holiest places in Islam and a place of deep meaning for millions of people around the world. I made this series to honor it.

The paintings show the golden dome in many moods: calm nights, red skies, and moments of fire. Some show the people who love this place, especially children. The series is a hope that one day these skies will be quiet.
